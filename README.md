## 🚀 RCC-AI

Many AI systems make decisions immediately, while the real-world outcomes needed to verify those decisions may arrive much later.

RCC-AI focuses on reliable state coordination across that delay — helping AI workflows remain consistent under concurrency, retries, crashes, replay, and stale updates.

A working transactional prototype has been developed and technically validated under concurrent and failure-recovery scenarios.

The underlying invention is currently the subject of a **PCT international patent application**.
